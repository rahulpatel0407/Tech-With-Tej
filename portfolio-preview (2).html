01 — About

Why this work.

I got into this because I like systems that have to actually work — not demo well, work. My first real taste of that was at IOCL, watching refinery engineers make calls off sensor dashboards I helped build. If the anomaly detection missed something, that wasn't an abstract metric — it was an operational risk on a live refinery floor.

Cybersecurity followed naturally. It's the same instinct pointed at a different kind of signal: instead of catching a faulty sensor, you're catching an intrusion before it becomes an incident. At NRSC, I'm doing exactly that — building analytics for anomalous activity in a live enterprise environment.

AI is the tool that makes both possible at scale. AgriHealth AI came from the same idea, applied somewhere it mattered more personally: smallholder farmers who lose a season to a disease they couldn't catch in time.

AgriHealth AI

IBM Hackathon 2026 Winner

A CNN-based crop-disease detector built for farmers with patchy connectivity — fine-tuned on domain-specific imagery, benchmarked for edge deployment, and pitched directly to Bihar's Principal Secretary (Agriculture).

10,000+Potential Users

Scan to explore AgriHealth AI

The Problem

Smallholder farmers often lose a season to a crop disease they catch too late. No affordable real-time diagnosis without reliable internet.

Approach

Fine-tuned CNN on domain-specific agricultural imagery. ~35% reduction in misclassification via systematic ablation studies.

Architecture

Compared edge vs. cloud inference across 4 IoT hardware configs. Optimized for sub-3s end-to-end response without cloud round-trip.

Challenges

Accuracy vs. latency vs. hardware cost. Every experiment was a response to that tension, not an optional extra.

Recognition

IBM Hackathon 2026 Winner. Presented at AI Summit Bihar 2026. Formal appreciation from Bihar's Education Minister and the Public Health Engineering Department Minister.

The Problem

Manual PPE compliance checks on an industrial floor don't scale and don't catch violations in real time.

Approach

YOLOv8, benchmarked against 3 baseline detection architectures to validate the choice before committing.

Challenges

Holding sub-200ms latency while keeping precision above 91% meant tuning confidence thresholds, NMS parameters, and input resolution together, not independently.
